At Samtrafiken, I worked on systems that sit at the core of Swedish public transportation infrastructure — connecting operators, passengers and vendors across the country.

This included building middleware solutions that controlled the fintech layer between major suppliers like SJ and other vendors, combining multi-operator ticketing into unified products like Resplus, and delivering open APIs that the entire industry relied on through Trafiklab.

I also contributed to the development and maintenance of Resrobot — one of Sweden's most used journey planners — ensuring reliability for millions of daily users.
